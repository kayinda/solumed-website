Make it a phone app (works offline)

Open the app link once while online - after that it runs with no internet. To get an app icon on your home screen:

📱 Android (Chrome)

Open the app, tap the ⋮ menu (top-right) → Add to Home screen (or Install app) → Add. An icon appears like any other app.

🍎 iPhone / iPad (Safari)

Open the app, tap the Share button (□↑ at the bottom) → Add to Home Screen → Add.

💾 Your data stays on that device. Use the app's Backup option now and then and keep the file safe - that's your only copy.
