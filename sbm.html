What's inside

One app, reshaped for eight trades

Choose a business type and the app hides what you don't need and surfaces what you do — a salon never sees drug-expiry tracking; a mobile-money agent gets a float ledger instead of a stockroom.

▤

Point of Sale

Fast search or barcode scan, cart with quantity editing, discounts, VAT, cash / mobile-money / card, and printed & reprintable receipts.

▦

Inventory & expiry

Stock levels, reorder alerts, suppliers, CSV import, plus batch & expiry tracking with "expiring soon" alerts for groceries and pharmacies.

⟳

Mobile-money agent banking

A full float module: deposits, withdrawals, transfers across MTN / Airtel / bank, auto-commission from built-in tariff tables, day-close float check.

📇

Debtors book

Sell on credit and track exactly who owes you, record part-payments, and clear debts — replacing the exercise book every shop keeps.

◉

Reports to send

Day / week / month P&L, top sellers, balance sheet, and a WhatsApp-ready business report the owner sends in one tap.

◨

Multi-till over Wi-Fi

Several tills in one shop share one live stock and one combined report over the local network — no cloud, no internet needed.

☁

NEW — SBM Premium: one shop on many devices

Create one cloud account for the shop and sign every till into it — devices sync over the internet even when each is on its own mobile data. Sell on one till, stock updates on all of them in seconds. 14-day free trial included.

☁

Watch from anywhere

An owner's private online dashboard shows today, this week, this month, or any date range — with a downloadable PDF. Monitor the shop from your phone, wherever you are. The till stays fully offline; it just checks in when it finds internet.

🛡

Cloud backup & restore

Back the whole system up online in one tap. If a device is lost, stolen or dies, restore everything onto a new one — your business is never trapped on one machine.

✂

Services & staff commission

For salons, clinics, guesthouses and workshops: sell services, and split one job's commission across several staff at their own rates — the barber and the stylist each get their cut of the same haircut.
