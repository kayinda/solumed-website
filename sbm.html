What's inside

One app, reshaped for eight trades

Choose a business type and the app hides what you don't need. Tap any tile for the detail.

▤Point of SaleScan, sell, print

Fast search or barcode scan, cart with quantity editing, discounts, VAT, cash / mobile-money / card, and printed & reprintable receipts.

▦Inventory & expiryStock, reorder, expiry

Stock levels, reorder alerts, suppliers, CSV import, plus batch & expiry tracking with "expiring soon" alerts for groceries and pharmacies.

⟳Agent bankingFloat and commission

A full float module: deposits, withdrawals, transfers across MTN / Airtel / bank, auto-commission from built-in tariff tables, day-close float check.

📇Debtors bookWho owes you what

Sell on credit and track exactly who owes you, record part-payments, and clear debts, replacing the exercise book every shop keeps.

◉Reports to sendP&L and WhatsApp

Day / week / month P&L, top sellers, balance sheet, and a WhatsApp-ready business report the owner sends in one tap.

◨Multi-till on Wi-FiMany tills, one stock

Several tills in one shop share one live stock and one combined report over the local network, with no cloud and no internet needed.

☁Premium syncAll devices as one shop

Create one cloud account for the shop and sign every till into it. Devices sync over the internet even when each is on its own mobile data. Included in the Full licence.

👁Watch from anywhereOwner dashboard online

A private online dashboard shows today, this week, this month or any date range, with a downloadable PDF. The till stays offline and checks in when it finds internet.

🛡Cloud backupNever lose the shop

Back the whole system up online in one tap. If a device is lost, stolen or dies, restore everything onto a new one.

✂Services & commissionSalons, clinics, workshops

Sell services and split one job's commission across several staff at their own rates. The barber and the stylist each get their cut of the same haircut.
