Solumed Pharmacy Manager

This page is shared privately with pilot partners and funders. Please do not post the link publicly.

Solumed Pharmacy Manager is a till built for pharmacies and drug shops: sales, stock, batch and expiry tracking, debtors and reports, with the built-in Solumed Steward, which gives traffic-light antibiotic guidance at the moment of sale and records what was sold.

Research preview, in validation. The Steward guidance comes from our Rx Steward research work and has not yet been validated in the field. It guides and records; the seller and the prescriber always decide. Please use it for review, demonstration and supervised pilots only.

Download

⬇ Windows ⬇ Android (APK) ⬇ macOS

Every copy starts with a full 14-day trial. On first run, choose Open demo shop to see a sample pharmacy with stock, sales and Steward records already in place. First-time login: admin / admin123.

Installing

  • Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because we are a new publisher. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager. Allow it for this install only.
  • macOS may say it cannot check the app. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
