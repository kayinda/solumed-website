💊 Rx Steward

Antimicrobial stewardship at the point of care

● In development

Rx Steward puts guideline-aligned empiric therapy, Uganda-verified resistance data and WHO AWaRe classification at the point of prescribing — currently covering 75 clinical syndromes with adult and paediatric dosing.

Under active development and validation — offered as a working research preview, not yet a registered medical device. We welcome pilot sites and research partners.
