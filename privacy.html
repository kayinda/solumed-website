Privacy policy

For Solumed Business Manager (Windows, Mac, Android and the Microsoft Store version) and solumedplus.com. Last updated 23 November 2026.

Who we are

Solumed Plus Limited, Kampala, Uganda. Contact: info@solumedplus.com or WhatsApp 0775 377 245.

Your business records stay on your device

Solumed Business Manager is an offline app. Your sales, stock, customers, debtors, staff and other business records are saved in a database file on your own computer or phone. We do not receive them unless you switch on one of the online features below.

What the app sends to us

  • Trial check. When the app first connects to the internet it sends an app code, a device identifier and a hardware fingerprint, so the free trial is counted once per device. No names, phone numbers or business records are sent.
  • Update check. The app asks our server for the latest version number. The Microsoft Store version leaves updates to the Store.
  • Online dashboard (only if you switch it on). The app sends a summary of your business figures, such as daily sales, products and stock levels, and staff names for the staff performance view, so you can see them on your phone. Customer names and phone numbers are not included.
  • Cloud backup and the web till (only if you switch them on). A copy of your business database is stored on our server, protected by your shop's private token, so you can restore it or work from a browser.

Buying a licence

Licences are bought on solumedplus.com. Card and mobile money payments are handled by Pesapal; we receive the payment confirmation, your name, email and phone number, and the device code of the computer you are licensing. We never see your card number or mobile money PIN.

Where data is stored and for how long

Online data is stored on our server in Germany (Hetzner), behind Cloudflare. We keep it while your shop uses the online features and delete it when you ask us to. Trial records are kept so the trial cannot restart on the same device.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show advertising in the app, or share your business records with anyone, except where the law requires it.

Website

solumedplus.com uses Google Analytics and GoatCounter to count visits and downloads. Enquiry forms send us the details you type in.

Your choices

You can use the app fully offline. You can switch the online dashboard, cloud backup and web till off at any time, and ask us to delete your online data or trial record by writing to info@solumedplus.com.
