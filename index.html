About us

Solumed Plus Limited

We sit at the intersection of software engineering, clinical & research expertise, and operational logistics — placing the right tool in the right hand at the right moment, across Uganda and beyond.

Vision

To be East Africa's most trusted builder of business solutions and health technologies that close the gap between evidence and practice.

Mission

Solumed Plus Limited supplies and delivers goods of all kinds, develops scalable business software, runs a health-technology research & innovation arm, and provides reliable logistics — across Uganda and beyond.

What we are registered to do

Health & clinical software and decision-support tools · health informatics, AI and data-analytics research · health & AMR consulting, M&E and scientific writing · pharmaceutical and medical-product distribution (NDA-licensed) · general supply, procurement and tendering · transport & logistics.
